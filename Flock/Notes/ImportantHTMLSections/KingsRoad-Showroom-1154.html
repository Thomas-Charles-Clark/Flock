INTERIOR DESIGN SERVICE

King's Road Fabric & Wallcovering Showroom

The King's Road store is where the story began for Creative Director and Founder Tricia Guild, as she explains - "I was working as an interior designer and was utterly frustrated at the lack of exciting and interesting contemporary textiles available, this led me to our first collection, a group of small block printed designs inspired by my great passion for India, and the brand has grown form that point to where we are today. Today the showroom is home to the brand's extensive range of fabrics, wallpapers, trimmings and paints, as well as the fabrics and wallpaper from all the brands that we distribute. Whatever you are looking for, be it fabric or wallcoverings; the perfect plains, wide width textiles, intricate velvets, embossed, vinyl wallpapers, printed fabrics in a myriad of scales and colours... this is the place to start."

Our friendly and experiences showroom staff and design consultants are there to guide you through the many collections with their first hand expert knowledge of the Designers Guild collections.
