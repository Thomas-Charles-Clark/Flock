King's Road Homestore

The Homestore was borne out of Creative Director and Founder Tricia Guild’s passion and ambition to create a complete and contemporary lifestyle. Walk through the doors and discover exquisite products for the home, each personally sourced and carefully considered to blend in and add another dimension to the DG story. It is here where the true incarnation of Guild’s vision can be seen and it occupies a unique place in London’s shopping scene.

Discover the full range of furniture, bed and bath and accessories that are all designed in the brand’s own design studio, presented alongside the many products sourced and edited by Tricia Guild - from antique and vintage furniture, lighting, jewellery, ceramics and glassware to stationary, books and garden furniture, as well as the best of contemporary and modern furniture and accessories from France, Italy and Scandinavia, and an extensive range from leading brands such as: Knoll, Vitra, Fritz Hansen, B&B Italia, Moroso and Cappellini, with lighting from Flos, Lieux, Artemide, Bestlite and Rugs from Kasthall.
